Questions before you start

All questions
How do I use SilaRex?

Take 10 drops (0.5 ml) daily, mixed into a quarter glass (50 to 70 ml) of water or fruit juice. Stir gently and drink immediately, preferably before breakfast. Shake the bottle first and do not take it undiluted.

How long does one bottle last?

One 30 ml bottle provides 60 servings, so two months at 10 drops a day. Once opened, use it within 4 months.

What is the difference between Women, Men and Active?

All three contain 4 mg of bioavailable silicon, 5 mg of zinc and 10 mg of choline chloride per 10 drops. Women adds folate and biotin. Men adds selenium and vitamin B6. Active adds manganese, vitamin B6 and vitamin B12.

Does SilaRex Active help with knee pain?

No, and we are not allowed to promise that either: a food supplement does not treat pain. The zinc and manganese in SilaRex Active do contribute to the maintenance of normal bones, and vitamin B12 to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue. If you have persistent knee pain, see your doctor.

Is SilaRex vegan and halal?

Yes. SilaRex is Vegan and Halal certified, contains no allergens and is free from artificial colours and sweeteners. It is suitable for vegetarians and vegans.

Can I use it if I am pregnant, breastfeeding or on medication?

Consult your healthcare practitioner before use.

What does bioavailable silicon mean?

The nutritional value of silicon depends not only on the amount present, but especially on its bioavailability. SilaRex supplies silicon as stabilised silicic acid, the biologically available form of silicon.
